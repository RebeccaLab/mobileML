Abstract
We are in the world where information is the ubiquitous but the authentication of that information is cumbersome. Deepfake
technology have achieved a tremendous growth in the digital era. Deepfake is a synthetic media audio, video or images that appear
to be realistic though they are fake or fabricated. Deepfake are created by Generative AI techniques that understand the probabilistic
distribution of the data. Appropriate detection systems are necessary to prevent the dissemination of misleading information and
guarantee the authenticity and integrity of data. Detecting deepfake contents in this digital era is very challenging due to the realistic
nature of fake images. In this paper, we present an enhanced CNN architecture, DeepGuardNet, a deepfake detection model that
is simple and effective for determining whether images are real or fake. DeepGuardNet is a straightforward, sequential, and robust
network designed for deepfake recognition and detection. Additionally, our network has an enhanced ability to detect tampered
content with fewer parameters due to the use of separable convolution. In this work, we utilize depthwise separable convolution
to efficiently extract deepfake features. The DeepGuardNet architecture effectively captures deepfake image features in both the
spatial and depth dimensions. Experimental study on Celeb-DF dataset demonstrated the competence of the proposed method with
an accuracy of 91% when compared with conventional methods. The proposed DeepGuardNet architecture is productive in terms
of the better feature extraction and reduced computational complexity.

3. Proposed Method- DeepGuardNet
In this segment, we present an efficient CNN architecture DeepGuardNet that is simple and light weight for en-
hanced deepfake detection. This simple model has the capability to extract fake features so that the architecture
becomes efficient. DeepGuardNet model is less complex, has less number of training parameters and reduced compu-
tational complexity. This network was designed by leveraging inspiration from MesoNet Proposed by Darius Afchar et
al[1]. In MesoNet, the mesoscopic feature which lies in between microscopic and macroscopic properties were exam-

ined and used for the manipulation of forged images. DeepGuardNet is utilizes the depth-wise separable convolutions
to decrease the computational complexity, less memory usage, less number of training parameters, . In addition to
the MesoNet layers, the model used one more convolution layer with separable convolution instead of the traditional
convolution method. As compared to the conventional methods it has less number of parameters which can facilitate
to prevent over fitting, which better the generalization to that has not been seen before the features extracted[2].
The DeepGuardNet have five consecutive separable convolution layers that inturn provide were used along with
batch normalization and max pooling layers. In this network the data in RGB color space is served by resizing it into
the frame size of 256 × 256 × 3 with three color channels, then passed into the convolutional layers. Here in separable
convolution the convolution is performed in two steps:a depth wise convolution followed by pointwise convolution.
Batch normalization technique is applied after each convolution for increasing its training speed and stability. For
reducing the spatial dimensions of the feature, map we use maxpooling layers that helps to capture the most relevant
features. To enhance their reliability, the convolutional layers uses ReLU activation function to bring in non-linearities
and normalize its output to avert vanishing gradient effect and the fully connected layers use Dropout to stabilize the
model and prevents over fitting. DeepGuardNet is a compact network having 3204 trainable parameters out of 3332
After the feature extraction process the model classifies the result into to real and fake images. Experiments are
conducted in Celeb-DF dataset and obtained better and efficient result as compared to others.

The dataset used in this work is Celeb-DF[18], which is a well known challenging dataset that makes a benchmark
dataset for accessing deepfake detection. It contains 5639 deepfake videos and 590 real videos. These videos have
average length of 13 seconds which have frame length of 30 frames per second. For training we have used 140002
and the validation set have 39428 image frame which are classified into real and forged images.The dataset has been

5. Conclusion
In the digital age, it is crucial to keep the integrity of data what we see and share in digital media. In this research,
we have developed an model DeepGuardNet for addressing the evolving challenge possessed by the AI generated false
contents in the digital media, that reduces the wide spread of the misinformation and safe guard the probity of online
media. We have used the mesoscopic features of an image for the detection with the help of separable convolution.
Using this method, results demonstrate that separable convolution is superior to the conventional method because of
its better performance. Separable convolution maintains or even improves the model with lesser number of parameters
and computational complexity. As comparing with the traditional models, we can state that separable convolution
can create a better impact on the forgery detection. Experimental results showcased a detection rate of 91% accuracy
on image frames of Celeb-DF dataset. The proposed model is efficient in identifying real and fake data that shows
its potential in realistic implementation in safeguarding the integrity of digital contents. Overall the use of separable
convolution has been found to be an imperative improvement, making a deepfake detection system more effective and
reliable. This work imposes a groundwork for the future events in deepfake detection resulting in further development
in this area and applied to variety of datasets and parameters.
